# EPIC-009: Club and partner relations

Generic organisation relationships for clubs, academies, associations, federations and sponsors. Support sport-independent opportunities, internal correspondence and staff-assessed relationship priority. Football is directory data/configuration for Moyours, not a mandatory sport in the model.

- US-009-01: sourced directory, private opportunities and interaction logging (development slice).
- Follow-up: controlled player submissions with sharing consent and approval, external correspondence integration, verified club portal and partnership-agreement lifecycle. These follow-up capabilities are not implemented or automatically included in the existing proposal baseline.
