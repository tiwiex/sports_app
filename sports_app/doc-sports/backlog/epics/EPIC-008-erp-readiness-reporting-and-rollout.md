# EPIC-008: ERP readiness, reporting and rollout

## Outcome

The academy launches with reconciled data and an explicit ERP boundary.

## Scope and success

- Retain ERP fit-gap, private operational/financial exports, reconciled migration and launch acceptance.
- Integrate procurement/receiving and player kit issue/return story into the main table, using native ERPNext stock/accounting controls.
- Merge approximately75 Excel/Zoho players, five-staff training, monthly/quarterly reporting, cloud delivery and support discovery.
